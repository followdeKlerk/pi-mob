# Privacy

pi-mob keeps execution on your host. Provider credentials, repositories, Pi sessions, and raw tool output are not sent to the mobile app unless needed for the selected view.

## Data handling

- The mobile app stores paired-host metadata, reconnectable session projections, and local drafts.
- The bridge stores durable command and event state on the host.
- Notifications are status-only by default and exclude prompts, answers, source paths, and tool output.
- Exports and attachments are private, host-managed resources with bounded retention.

## Your responsibilities

Use a private network, protect the host and phone with operating-system controls, and review what you share through the native share sheet. pi-mob does not provide cloud synchronization, public sharing links, or provider credential management on mobile.
